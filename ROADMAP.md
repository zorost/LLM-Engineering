# Roadmap

Public work remaining after the first publication of this repository.

## Keep current

- Refresh S9 and S10 when a new reasoning-training method becomes the
  default, not when it becomes a tweet.
- Refresh E12 when a major cloud changes its agent or eval product name.
- Refresh [reference/YOUTUBE.md](reference/YOUTUBE.md) each spring when
  Stanford CS336 posts a new playlist.

## Add when they earn a module

- A discrete-diffusion language-model module, if serving them becomes a
  production job rather than a paper.
- A native multimodal training module once open recipes stabilize.
- A federated or confidential-compute serving module if a public, legal
  tutorial exists that we can point at without copying.

## Will not add

- Restricted, research-only, or non-commercial weights presented as a
  house default.
- Exploit code, jailbreak catalogs, or credential harvesting.
- A hosted platform, a Discord requirement, or a paid cohort as a gate.

Training 01 remains [AI Engineering Lab](https://github.com/zorost/AI-Engineering-Lab).
This repository remains Training 02. Later trainings publish as their own
repos on [github.com/zorost](https://github.com/zorost) and as numbered
posts on [zorost.com/ai-lab/training](https://zorost.com/ai-lab/training).
