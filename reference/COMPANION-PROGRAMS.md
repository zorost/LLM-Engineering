## AI Engineering Lab (Training 01)

**What.** A 24-week, self-paced program from Python to a governed
production lakehouse. One fictional freight case study the whole
way. CPU-first notebooks. Databricks in the last phase.

**Where.**
[github.com/zorost/AI-Engineering-Lab](https://github.com/zorost/AI-Engineering-Lab)
· site: [zorost.com/ai-engineering-lab](https://zorost.com/ai-engineering-lab)
· browsable weeks: [zorost.github.io/AI-Engineering-Lab](https://zorost.github.io/AI-Engineering-Lab/)

**Use it when.** You need Python, eval habits, RAG, LoRA, agents,
cloud platforms, and Databricks as a **weekly cadence**, not as a
field encyclopedia. START-HERE of this course sends beginners
there for weeks 1 to 4. Study plan D is the return path.

**Do not use it as.** A substitute for Scientist depth (CS336,
S1 to S12) or for Operator SLOs (O1 to O4). Different job.

## Transformer Explainer

**What.** In-browser explanations of attention, RoPE, GQA, KV
cache, and MoE. Teaching instrument, not a server.

**Where.**
[github.com/zorost/transformer-explainer](https://github.com/zorost/transformer-explainer)

**Use it when.** S1 and labs 02, 17, 18. If a diagram in this
course feels static, open the explainer and move a slider.

## 30 days of Databricks

**What.** Lakehouse literacy: Unity Catalog, Spark, jobs, and
the objects Engineer E12 assumes you can name.

**Where.**
[github.com/zorost/30-days-of-Databricks](https://github.com/zorost/30-days-of-Databricks)

**Use it when.** You took the Engineer door, you will ship on
Databricks, and Training 01's last four weeks are more than you
need (or you want a second pass). Skip it if you will never
touch a lakehouse.

curriculum. Not marketing.

**Where.**
[zorost.com/ai-lab/fieldwork](https://zorost.com/ai-lab/fieldwork)

## Open source shelf

Public tools the Lab publishes under [github.com/zorost](https://github.com/zorost)
and lists at [zorost.com/ai-lab/open-source](https://zorost.com/ai-lab/open-source).
Names you will see on that shelf include document preparation
and reading tools (DocPrep-AI, MarkForge, OneRead), lecture-to-
text helpers (yt2textbook), lakehouse helpers (AlchemyLake),
and other MIT-licensed utilities as the register updates.

**Use them when.** A product job in L4 needs a pipeline you
would otherwise rebuild. Read each repo's license and README.
Do not treat a tool on the shelf as an internal serving recipe.
This course does not document private clusters.
